### E1 — FINRA daily short-volume signal — VERDICT: CONFIRM (build-narrowed)

**Finding:** Confirmed, with one citation correction — the proprietary-data paper is **Boehmer, Jones & Zhang (2008)**, not "Boehmer-Jones-Wu-Zhang."

- **(a) Access / format — solid.** FINRA's Daily Short Sale Volume File is a free, pipe-delimited text file with fields `Date|Symbol|ShortVolume|ShortExemptVolume|TotalVolume|Market` (Date = YYYYMMDD, volumes are integer share counts, Market = N/Q/B/D/O reporting-facility code). Posted no later than 6:00pm ET on the same trade date, downloadable as CSV (up to 7 days at a time, 365-day window), **no paid key**, and also available via a free Query API (CSV/JSON) at developer.finra.org for automation. Daily cadence, no key, easy to ingest — this part is fully verified.
- **(b) The edge is real but weak and NOT standalone-profitable.** Three primary sources settle it:
  1. **Diether, Lee & Werner 2009** (using the public SEC Reg SHO data, the same public family the free FINRA file derives from) found short sellers are contrarian and that a rise in relative short selling predicts a short-term **return reversal**: a 1-standard-deviation jump predicts a -2.90 bp next-day abnormal return (~7.52% annualized gross); a small-trade long-short portfolio earns ~3.0%/month, large short trades give an insignificant -16 bp/month.
  2. **Kelley & Tetlock 2017** (RFS) is the load-bearing "free data is too weak" evidence: the strong 9%-annualized informed edge comes ONLY from **proprietary retail short-order data**; the public aggregate Reg SHO short volume ("AllShort") is 0.99-correlated with institutional short flow, so the free file is effectively just institutional/total flow — the unique-information retail slice cannot be separated out.
  3. **Market-maker inflation** is confirmed by FINRA + OTC Markets: the daily file "conflates directional investors ... with market maker short selling activity ... into one confusing number," so a stock with healthy bona-fide MM activity can show large Daily Short Volumes.

**Load-bearing caveat (verbatim from DLW 2009 abstract):** *"A trading strategy based on daily short-selling activity generates significant returns, but incurs costs large enough to wipe out any profits."* The signal predicts statistically but is NOT net-profitable as a standalone trade — it is contaminated by MM hedging and too thin to trade alone. Exactly the profile of a **confluence-only modifier, never a standalone trigger**. NOTE: the file's `ShortExemptVolume` column lets MM/exempt shorts be partially netted out (`ShortVolume - ShortExemptVolume`), a cheap DLW-style quality improvement.

### E3 — GEX gamma-flip level — VERDICT: CONFIRM (build-narrowed)

**Finding:** The qualitative gamma-flip heuristic is real and consensus, but its predictive uniqueness is weak and free EOD data is coarse — so it survives ONLY as a labeled regime/level hint, not a signal or a precise number.

- **(a) Sign convention — sound heuristic, but contested on two fronts.** The directional claim ("dealers short gamma below the flip amplify moves; long above dampen") is the shared framing of every major practitioner (SpotGamma, SqueezeMetrics, Unusual Whales) AND has empirical backing: an 8-year SPY backtest found a strong raw link between net dealer gamma and next-day realized vol (Spearman rho = -0.36, p = 4.6e-60, n = 1,971 days). The mechanism is not made up. **However:**
  - (i) The sign convention is NOT universally agreed — SpotGamma assumes dealers long calls / short puts; older SqueezeMetrics-style convention assumes the inverse. Providers differ on expirations and grid resolution, so flip levels diverge (e.g. SpotGamma $430 vs SqueezeMetrics $435 — "both are approximations").
  - (ii) Dealer positioning is fundamentally unobservable: ~60-70% of options notional is OTC and invisible, so "absolute precision is impossible."
  - (iii) **MOST DAMAGING** — after controlling for VIX and ATM implied vol, the correlation collapses from -0.36 to -0.03 (p = 0.18), and the residualized top-minus-bottom realized-vol spread drops from -10.63 vol points to -0.99 (p = 0.25). Author's verdict: *"GEX is not fake. The raw effect is real. The incremental information after VIX and ATM IV is the part that fails... Use GEX as a regime label, not a standalone forecast."*
- **(b) Free yfinance EOD feasibility — computable but coarse/stale.** The formula (`gamma x OI x 100 x spot^2 x 0.01`) needs only gamma + open interest per strike, both of which yfinance returns, so a rough net-GEX and flip level ARE computable for free. Two real limits: (1) yfinance has documented missing/incomplete option-chain rows (GitHub issue #1847), biasing the sum; (2) open interest is an end-of-day snapshot that lags intraday positioning — worst for 0DTE, where contracts open and expire same-day without accumulating OI. SpotGamma itself runs a proprietary intraday adjustment because raw EOD OI reflects "yesterday's positioning."

**Load-bearing caveat:** Build it ONLY as an explicitly-labeled heuristic display field (gamma flip ≈ S/R, sign ≈ vol-amplify-below / vol-dampen-above), with a note that it lags intraday and is one of several disagreeing conventions. Do NOT present a precise number, do NOT treat it as predictive alpha beyond what VIX already says, and ideally cross-check direction against existing options-flow / implied-vol data rather than firing standalone.

### E6 — Manufactured-agreement / coordinated-burst defense — VERDICT: CONFIRM (build-narrowed)

**Finding:**

- **(a) Confirmed exactly.** arXiv 2512.02261 is *"TradeTrap: Are LLM-based Trading Agents Truly Reliable and Faithful?"* (posted Dec 1, 2025). Its Table 1 (data-fabrication results) contains all three claimed pairs: total return 11.59% (clean "News") drops to 5.26% ("News + Tampered"); Sharpe 4.34 → 3.20; max position concentration 77.13% under tampered condition. Attack described almost verbatim: *"injecting coordinated counterfeit narratives into the external news and social signal feeds... synchronized fake news events—ranging from scripted market crises to fabricated technological breakthroughs—to corrupt the informational layer while leaving the underlying numerical price paths unaltered."* **LOAD-BEARING CAVEAT:** those degradation numbers are for the **Adaptive agent only**. Procedural agents kept stable returns yet were STILL driven by the manipulated narrative — apparent robustness came from structural position limits, not genuine resistance.
- **(b) Confirmed by independent corroboration:** arXiv 2601.13082 ("Adversarial News and Lost Profits") shows headline manipulation cuts an LLM trading system's annual return by up to 17.7 percentage points; arXiv 2601.15277 shows LLM "sentiment attacks" drop fake-news-detector F1 by up to 21.51%; real-world coordinated pump-and-dump campaigns (CYNK, China Liberal Education indictment, 2026 Bloomberg investigation) plus coordinated-network detection research document multi-account/synchronized-timing fake narratives moving real markets.

**SECOND CAVEAT for the defense:** TradeTrap does NOT itself propose independent-corroboration or burst-detection as a mitigation, and does not state how many distinct sources the attack used — so the defense idea is supported by the threat literature, not lifted from this paper, and the paper implies a single coordinated narrative can suffice. A "needs 2+ independent sources" gate only helps if independence is genuine; a coordinated burst is engineered to fake exactly that independence, so the gate must **test source independence (distinct origins/timing), not just source count.** Do not over-claim it as "implementing the TradeTrap defense."
