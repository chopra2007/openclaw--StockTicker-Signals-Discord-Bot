## Learnings
- python3 only; Bash cwd persists. Full suite ~run in seconds; run_phase3 ~1-2 min (bootstraps).
- Canonical NYSE Zweig thresholds (0.40/0.615) DO NOT transfer to the broad-US ABI feed (fires
  6x in 16y, none post-2019). Self-normalizing percentile thrust (0.10->0.90 of trailing-252
  EMA percentile) is the honest adaptation — pre-registered as such.
- A breadth thrust must be gated by a RECENT washout (within ~25d), NOT same-day (thrust is the
  recovery surge that comes AFTER the low; same-day AND = 0 fires).
- The 92% bottom base-rate trap is robust: even a rare, theory-backed thrust can't beat "any
  deeply-pulled-back day" — the bottom side is base-rate-bound on free data, full stop.
